1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

1.2 The Problem Statement

Traditional Kirana store owners operate in high-density, noise-heavy environments with razor-thin margins. They face four fatal operational bottlenecks:

  • Manual Rush-Hour Billing: Writing paper slips or navigating nested POS software during peak morning/evening hours causes customer walkouts and unrecorded sales.
  • Uncollected Customer Credit (Udhaar): Customer credit is recorded in physical paper diaries (Khata). These diaries get lost, torn, or forgotten, causing significant unrecoverable debt.
  • Silent Working Capital Losses: Capital sits locked in slow-moving "dead stock" or items expiring on back shelves without warning.
  • The Digital Divide: Complex ERP software requires typing and barcode scanning, which traditional shopkeepers find too tedious and time-consuming.
The SmartDukaan Philosophy
"Existing billing apps force the merchant to do data entry. SmartDukaan listens, sees, and thinks proactively on behalf of the merchant."

3. End-to-End System Workflows

3.1 Vernacular Voice Billing Workflow

  1. Acoustic Input: The shopkeeper clicks the microphone button and speaks naturally in mixed Hindi/English: "Rahul ko do packet Parle-G aur ek packet Amul butter udhaar".
  2. Payload Ingestion: The browser streams text to `POST /api/v1/ai/voice-billing`.
  3. Intent & Entity Parsing:
    • Payment Mode: Detects "udhaar" $\to$ flags transaction as credit.
    • Customer Entity: Identifies "Rahul" from the customer database.
    • Item & Quantity: Matches "Parle-G" (Qty: 2, Unit: pkt) and "Amul butter" (Qty: 1, Unit: pkt).
  4. Catalog Pricing Lookup: Queries unit sell prices from the product table and calculates tax, subtotal, and total in under 800 ms.
  5. Transaction Commitment: On user approval, `POST /api/v1/sales/` deducts stock and appends a debit entry to Rahul's digital ledger.

3.2 Digital Khata Book & Credit Risk Workflow

  1. Customer Ledger Management: Every customer profile maintains credit limits, current balance, and repayment velocity.
  2. Credit Transactions: GIVEN increases pending debt; RECEIVED reduces outstanding balance.
  3. Dynamic Trust Tiering:
    • Platinum: Repays within 7 days, utilization < 40%.
    • Good: Repays within 20 days, utilization < 70%.
    • High Risk: Repayment overdue > 30 days, utilization > 90%.
  4. WhatsApp Reminder Dispatch: With one click, generates a customized, polite vernacular reminder with payment links: "Namaste Rahul ji, aapke Sharma General Store par ₹420 baaki hain. Kripya samay par jama karein."

3.3 Wholesaler Invoice OCR Scanner Workflow

  1. Image Acquisition: Storekeeper snaps a photo of the distributor's paper bill via BillScan.jsx.
  2. VLM / OCR Ingestion: Sent to `POST /api/v1/ai/scan-bill`.
  3. Table Extraction: Detects distributor name, bill number, line items, batch codes, wholesale cost price, and suggested retail price.
  4. One-Click Restocking: Clicking "Apply to Inventory" updates stock balances automatically without manual typing.

3.4 Autonomous Proactive Intelligence Workflow

The proactive engine runs automatically on server startup or when triggered via `POST /api/v1/insights/recalculate`. It executes four distinct diagnostic routines:

  • Expiry Risk Ranking: Identifies products with expiries < 30 days. Calculates total capital at risk ($\text{Stock} \times \text{Cost Price}$) and suggests promotional bundles.
  • Dead Stock Detection: Flags items with stock > 10 units that have recorded zero sales over the past 20 days.
  • Smart Reorder Alerts: Identifies fast-moving stock falling below safe threshold levels (`min_stock`).
  • Cash-flow Forecasting: Projects weekend liquidity by balancing anticipated cash inflows against supplier payables.
